Why No Escrow License Is Required
DirtBidder uses Stripe Connect — a licensed money transmitter in all 50 U.S. states — to hold and transfer all funds. DirtBidder itself never holds, stores, or transmits your money directly. We act as an escrow administrator: we instruct Stripe when to hold, release, or refund funds based on job status and disputes. This is the same model used by Airbnb, Uber, Fiverr, and thousands of other marketplaces. DirtBidder LLC does not need a broker's license, escrow license, or money transmitter license to operate this system.
