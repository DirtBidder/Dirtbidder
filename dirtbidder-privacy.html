Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Information You Give Us

We collect the information You provide when You create an Account, post or bid on a Job, send a message, or make or receive a payment, including:

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, password (which We store in scrambled form and cannot read), company name, and ZIP code. For Operators, We also collect service radius, equipment, and years of experience.
  • Job details: the Job description, the site address and map location, acreage, budget, timeline, and photographs the Client uploads.
  • Messages, bids, and change requests sent between users, including attachments and photographs, and the explanations and photographs submitted in a dispute over held funds.
  • Reviews and ratings.
  • Records of Your acceptance of our Terms of Service, including the date and the version You accepted.

Payment Information

Payments and Operator payouts are processed by Stripe, Inc. Stripe collects and handles all card and bank account details directly, under its own privacy policy. We never see or store full card or bank account numbers. We keep records of payment amounts, fees, dates, and status, and records of Operator earnings for tax reporting.

Usage Data

When You visit the Website, Our own server records the page You viewed and the domain of the website that referred You. We do not store Your IP address for this purpose. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs, which may include IP addresses, which We use to keep the Service secure and working. To count unique visitors, We use a scrambled visitor code that changes every day. We do not use Google Analytics or any other outside analytics service.

Tracking Technologies and Cookies

The Website uses Your browser’s storage to operate the Service, and the Meta Pixel uses Cookies and similar technologies to measure our advertising. The technologies We use may include:

  • Cookies or Browser Cookies. A cookie is a small file placed on Your Device. You can instruct Your browser to refuse all Cookies or to indicate when a Cookie is being sent. However, if You do not accept Cookies, You may not be able to use some parts of our Service.
  • Pixels, Tags, and Web Beacons. Small pieces of code or tiny image files placed on the Website that tell Us, or an Advertising Partner, that a page was viewed or an action was taken.

We use the following categories of Cookies and tracking technologies:

  • Browser storage. Our Website does not set cookies of its own. It saves Your login, one display setting, and, if You use “Fill in from my last job,” the details of Your last posted Job (Job type, measurements, description, budget, and equipment, but not the address, location, or photographs) in Your browser’s local storage. Anyone using that browser may see these saved details. Clearing it will log You out and remove them.
  • Meta Pixel cookies. The only cookies on the Website are set by the Meta Pixel, including a cookie stored under our Website’s domain that Meta uses to measure our ads.
  • Advertising (Meta Pixel). Administered by Meta Platforms, Inc. The Meta Pixel tells Meta when You view a page on the Website, create an Account (and whether as a Client or an Operator), post a Job, watch a demo video, or view our Oklahoma page, along with Your IP address and browser information. We do not send Meta Your name, email address, payment information, or Job details through the Pixel. We use this to measure our ads and to show ads for DirtBidder to people who have visited the Website or who are similar to them. Meta’s use of this information is governed by Meta’s own privacy policy.
  • Customer-list and lookalike audiences. We may upload a list of Our Operators’ email addresses, phone numbers, first and last names, ZIP codes, and country to Meta. The list is scrambled (hashed) before it is matched to Meta accounts. Meta uses the matches to show Our ads to those Operators and to people with similar characteristics (“lookalike audiences”).
  • Content from other providers. Pages on the Website load fonts from Google Fonts, map images from OpenStreetMap (displayed with the Leaflet library from unpkg.com), and a QR-code tool from cdnjs (Cloudflare). When Your browser loads these, the provider receives Your IP address and browser information, and OpenStreetMap receives the map area being shown.

We currently advertise on Meta (Facebook and Instagram). We may also advertise through Google, including YouTube, using similar tools, in which case Google may collect the same kinds of information described above.

Your Choices About Cookies and Advertising

  • You can set Your browser to block or delete Cookies.
  • You can control the ads You see on Facebook and Instagram through Your Meta account settings, including Meta’s ad preferences.
  • You can opt out of interest-based advertising from many participating companies through the Digital Advertising Alliance at https://optout.aboutads.info and the Network Advertising Initiative at https://optout.networkadvertising.org.

Opting out does not mean You will stop seeing ads. It means the ads You see will be less tailored to You.

The Company may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To manage Your Account.

  • To analyze and improve the Service, including understanding how users find and use the Website.
  • To advertise the Service, including measuring and targeting our ads through Advertising Partners as described above.

How Information Is Shared Between Users

Because the Service connects Clients and Operators, some of Your information is shown to other users:

  • Before a Job is hired, Operators can see the Job title and description, Job type, town and ZIP code, acreage, timeline, budget, site photographs, number of bids, and date posted. They do not see the Client’s name, the exact address, or the map pin unless the Client chooses to share the location.
  • A Client may choose to share the exact Job location with one or more Operators through the message thread, and may stop sharing it at any time.
  • Once a Job is hired and the first payment is funded, the exact site address goes to the hired Operator, and the Client and Operator can see each other’s phone number and email address.
  • Operator profiles are visible only to logged-in users and show the company name (or the Operator’s name if there is no company), member-since date, equipment, years of experience, service radius, a short bio, star rating, badges, and reviews. Each review shows the reviewer’s first name and the Job title.
  • The exact location of a Job site is used only to run that Job, including sharing it with the Operators the Client chooses and the hired Operator, and to resolve disputes about the Job. We do not send it to Meta or use it for advertising or analytics. If the hired Operator taps “Get Directions,” the address opens in Google Maps, and Google receives it under its own privacy policy.

Messages

Messages on the Service are between users, but they are not private from Us. We may access, review, scan, filter, store, and retain messages and attachments, and We use automated tools that may flag, hold, or block content. We do this to resolve disputes, to enforce our Terms of Service, to prevent fraud, to keep people safe, and to comply with the law.

Retention of Your Personal Data

We keep Personal Data only as long as needed for the purposes in this Privacy Policy, to comply with Our legal obligations, to resolve disputes, and to enforce Our agreements. As a general guide:

  • Account details are kept while Your Account is open and for three years after it is closed.
  • Jobs, bids, messages, photographs, change requests, and dispute records are kept for three years after the Job is completed or closed, so that disputes and claims about a Job, and our Terms of Service about keeping Jobs on the Service, can be resolved.
  • Payment records and Operator earnings are kept for seven years for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Records of Your acceptance of our Terms of Service are kept for as long as We keep Your Account information.
  • Usage Data is kept for 26 months, except where it is used to strengthen security or We are legally required to keep it longer.
  • Records of referrals, including who We introduced to a supplier and what the supplier reported back, are kept for seven years with Our payment records.

Closing Your Account does not delete information We must keep under this section. When We no longer need Personal Data, We delete it or remove the details that identify You.

These periods are the longest We keep each type of information. If You make a deletion request under “Your Privacy Rights,” We will keep only what We need for the purposes listed there.

Supplier Referrals

We may introduce You to suppliers such as equipment dealers and rental companies, quarries, culvert and tile suppliers, and manufacturers, and a supplier may pay Us a fee when someone We introduced buys from it. This happens in two ways.

  • Referral links. Some pages carry a link or code that takes You to a supplier’s own website. The supplier learns that You came from the Service. We do not send the supplier Your Personal Data when You simply follow a link.
  • “Send my info.” Where We offer it, You can ask Us to send Your name and contact details, and the type of work You are asking about, to a named supplier so that it can give You a quote. We send this only when You ask Us to, and only to the supplier You choose. Once it is sent, that supplier holds the information as its own and uses it under its own privacy policy. You deal with the supplier directly, and We are not a party to anything You buy from it.

A supplier We introduced You to may tell Us whether You bought from it, and the date and amount, so that We can work out the fee We are owed, and We may ask a supplier to let Us check its records of those sales. We use this to administer the referral program and for Our own accounting and tax records.
