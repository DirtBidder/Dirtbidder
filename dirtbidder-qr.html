How to Get Cards Printed
1
Download your QR code using the PNG button above. It's print-ready at any size.
2
Download the business card design and take it to a print shop or upload to an online printer.
3
Recommended printers: VistaPrint.com, GotPrint.com, or Canva Print — all accept PNG/PDF files. 500 cards typically cost $20–$40.
4
For truck decals or signs: Take the downloaded PNG to any local sign shop. Ask for a weatherproof vinyl decal — they'll size it to fit.
5
Test it first — scan your QR code with your phone before printing to confirm it goes to the right URL.
