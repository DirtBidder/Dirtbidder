How it works

  1. ApplyTell us what you sell and where. We review every partner before they go live.
  2. Sign the partner agreementOne simple agreement, accepted online.
  3. Get your link and codeEvery customer we send you is tracked with your own link and code.
  4. Show up on matching jobsYour company appears on the jobs that need what you sell, in the states you serve.
  5. Get quote requestsInterested customers send their name, phone and town through DirtBidder, and we pass them straight to you.
  6. Pay only on salesYou send us a monthly list of sales to customers we referred, and pay the referral fee on those.

What it costs

Nothing to join, and nothing unless a customer we send you buys. The referral fee depends on what you sell:

What you sellReferral fee
Storm shelter under $5,000$300 per sale
Storm shelter $5,000 to $10,000$500 per sale
Storm shelter over $10,000Set in your agreement
Tile, rock and other supplies2 to 3% of the order

A customer counts as our referral for 12 months after we send them to you, however they end up buying. Customers who find you on their own are always yours.
