📋 Notes for Your Accountant
DirtBidder LLC is a single-member LLC — income flows to your personal return on Schedule C (unless you elect S-Corp status)
Platform commission revenue is classified as service income / marketplace fees — not sales of goods
Referral income from Cat Financial and rental partners may be classified as referral/affiliate income — confirm with your CPA
Stripe will issue a 1099-K for payment processing volume — this reflects gross transaction volume, NOT your net income. Do not confuse the two.
All operator payouts flow through Stripe Connect — DirtBidder does not issue 1099s to operators directly; Stripe handles that for payouts over $600
Consider quarterly estimated tax payments to avoid underpayment penalties — typically 25–30% of net income set aside
Technology expenses (hosting, domain, software) and home office may be additional deductible items — discuss with your CPA
Export Options
All exports include your DirtBidder LLC name, EIN, and the tax year. Keep downloaded copies in your business records for at least 7 years. DirtBidder recommends using a licensed CPA for your annual tax filing.
